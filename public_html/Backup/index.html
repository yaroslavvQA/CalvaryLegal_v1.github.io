Our Approach

Precision Over Templates.
Strategy Over Forms.

Every engagement at Calvary International Law begins not with a form or a checklist, but with a thorough understanding of who you are, where you've been, and where you intend to go.

I
Comprehensive Discovery
We begin every engagement with a thorough analysis of your full picture — assets, residency history, citizenship, family structure, and intentions. No strategy is built without a complete foundation.
II
Multi-Jurisdictional Coordination
We don't advise in a vacuum. Our planning is coordinated with your accountants, foreign counsel, and financial advisors — ensuring every piece of the structure works together across every jurisdiction that applies to you.
III
Bespoke Structuring
We design structures that fit your life — not generic templates adapted to your situation. Each engagement produces a tailored strategy aligned with your legal obligations, risk tolerance, and long-term goals.
IV
Ongoing Stewardship
International law changes. Your life changes. We monitor both — providing annual reviews, law-change alerts, and proactive updates to keep your structures legally sound and strategically optimal.
Our engagements begin with a thorough analysis — not a template.

Yonathan Amselem founded Calvary International Law PLLC to deliver the caliber of international legal counsel previously available only to the largest institutional clients — with the discretion, accessibility, and personalized attention that complex cross-border matters demand.

His practice focuses on the intersection of international tax law, cross-border estate planning, and global wealth structuring. He advises individuals, families, and business owners navigating the legal complexities that arise when life, assets, and ambitions span multiple jurisdictions.

Every matter at Calvary International Law receives Mr. Amselem's direct, senior-level attention — not delegated to associates or paralegals. Clients engage directly with the attorney who holds the expertise, builds the strategy, and is accountable for the result.
