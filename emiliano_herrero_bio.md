**Emiliano Herrero**
Chief Operating Officer · Calvary International Law PLLC

Emiliano Herrero serves as Chief Operating Officer at Calvary International Law, where he leads the firm's operations and business strategy. He oversees the financial, administrative, and growth functions that allow the firm's legal team to focus entirely on client matters.

Mr. Herrero is a CFA charterholder with fifteen years of experience in finance, including credit analysis within institutional risk teams. That work gave him direct exposure to how lenders and counterparties evaluate the structures clients bring to the table — and a fluency in the financial and structural concepts at the core of Calvary's practice.

Mr. Herrero is not an attorney and does not provide legal advice. His role is to ensure that Calvary operates with the institutional discipline of a much larger firm — that records are precise, communications are responsive, and clients experience the firm as organized and reliable.
