Engagement Framework

International planning is not a product — it is a process.

01
Discovery & Situation Analysis
A comprehensive review of your tax residency, citizenship, domicile, asset locations, entity holdings, and family structure across every relevant jurisdiction. No strategy is built without a complete foundation.
Residency Analysis Asset Mapping Treaty Review Compliance Audit
02
Structure Design
We select optimal jurisdictions and entity types — trusts, foundations, LLCs, corporations — and design the strategic blueprint tailored to your objectives, tax profile, and family circumstances.
Trust & Entity Design Tax Modeling Jurisdiction Selection

Foreign Counsel Documentation Entity Formation Account Setup

Cross-border structures require annual attention — FBAR filings, Form 3520 reporting, and periodic review as laws change. We provide ongoing counsel and coordinate with your accounting team year after year.
FBAR / FATCA Form 3520 Annual Reviews Law Change Monitoring
