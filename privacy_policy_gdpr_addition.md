## European Union and United Kingdom Data Protection Rights

If you are accessing our website or providing information to us from the European Union, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR"). This section describes those rights and how we handle your personal information.

### Data Controller

Calvary International Law PLLC is the data controller for personal information collected through our website and in the course of providing legal services. Inquiries about how we handle your personal information can be directed to:

### Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information on one or more of the following legal bases:

- **Consent.** Where you have voluntarily provided information through our website or through written consent during onboarding.
- **Performance of a contract.** Where processing is necessary to evaluate or carry out an engagement with you, or to take steps at your request before entering into one.
- **Legitimate interests.** Including responding to inquiries, conducting conflicts checks, securing our systems, and operating the firm, balanced against your rights and freedoms.
- **Legal obligation.** Where processing is required to comply with applicable law or rules of professional conduct.

### Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you and obtain a copy of it.
- **Rectify** information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Erase** your personal information, subject to recordkeeping obligations that apply to law firms and to the defense of legal claims.
- **Restrict** how we process your information in certain circumstances.
- **Object** to processing that is based on our legitimate interests.
- **Receive** your personal information in a portable, machine-readable format, and to have it transmitted to another controller where technically feasible.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the firm using the information above. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

### International Data Transfers

Because Calvary International Law is based in the United States, personal information you provide to us is transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws differ from those in the European Union and United Kingdom. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms approved under applicable law.

### Right to Complain to a Supervisory Authority

If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence or place of work. We would, however, appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns directly. Please contact the firm before lodging a complaint where practical.

### Automated Decision-Making

We do not engage in automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects regarding visitors to our website or clients of the firm.
